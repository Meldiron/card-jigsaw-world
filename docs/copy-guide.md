# Card Jigsaw World — UI copy guide

Voice: calm, warm, precise. A good friend who knows the game. No hype, no exclamation stacks, no "awesome".

Terms (always these, always this case): Coins, Tickets, Hints, puzzle, pack, board, card, group, lock, daily puzzle, daily gift, streak, level, VIP, Lucky Wheel, piggy bank, quests, events.

- **Quests** is the tab and the word for the daily and weekly tasks ("Daily quests", "Weekly quests"). Do not say "goals" in the UI; the code still calls them `Goal`/`Goals`.
- **Events** are the world-day event packs ("Live events" on the Quests tab and Home).

CTAs start with a verb and name the outcome:
- Play · Continue · Replay · Next puzzle · Unlock for 2,400 (with coin icon) · Go VIP · Claim 60 coins · Spin for 1 ticket · Open piggy bank · Save progress · Create account · Sign in · Watch video
- Never "OK", "Submit", "Yes". Dialog buttons name the action: "Delete photo" / "Keep".

Status lines are short noun phrases: "Ready to claim", "Back tomorrow", "3 of 5 left today", "Opens in 2d 5h", "Ends in 3d", "Claimed", "Solved · 3 stars".

Empty states: what this is + why empty + how to start, in two short sentences. Errors: what happened + how to fix, no blame.

Numbers: use the Amount widget (icon + number) instead of words where space is tight; otherwise "400 coins".

Celebration is restrained: "Picture complete", "Level 3", "Streak: 4 days". One line, then the numbers.
