# Support

Card Jigsaw World is made by one person. Questions and bug reports go to **support@almostapps.eu**. Replies usually take a day or two.

## Frequently asked questions

### I bought coins, tickets, hints or VIP and nothing arrived

Purchases are confirmed by Apple first and then verified by our server, which normally takes a few seconds. If the balance does not update:

1. Check that the device is online and give it a moment.
2. Open Profile > Settings > Account > Restore purchases.
3. If it still is not there, send us the date of the purchase and the order id from your Apple receipt email. We will credit it by hand.

### How do I restore purchases on a new phone?

Sign in with the same account (Apple or email) and open Profile > Settings > Account > Restore purchases. VIP follows your Apple ID, so it is restored even if you play as a guest on the new device.

### I lost my progress

Progress belongs to the account that was signed in when you played.

- If you played as a guest and reinstalled the app or changed phones, the guest account on the old installation cannot be recovered automatically. Write to us with your old friend code (Social > Friends > Add friend, under "Your code") and your new one, and we will move the profile across.
- If you had an email account or Sign in with Apple, just sign in again from Profile > Settings > Account.

To avoid this in the future, create an account from Profile > Settings > Account. It keeps your guest progress.

### How do I sign in?

Profile > Settings > Account offers Sign in with Apple and email sign-up. Both keep the progress you already have as a guest. There is no password reset for guest accounts because they have no password; for email accounts use "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen.

### How do I cancel VIP?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside the game:

1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
3. Choose Card Jigsaw World and tap Cancel.

VIP stays active until the end of the period you already paid for. Cancelling during the 7-day free trial means you are never charged. Refunds are requested from Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### What does VIP include?

Every pack, including every past monthly daily pack back to January 2023, and no store promotions inside the game. Coins, tickets and hints are still earned the usual way.

### Why do some pictures load slowly or not at all?

Pictures are loaded from their original sources (Openverse, the Cleveland Museum of Art, NASA, Lorem Picsum). A slow connection or a source that is temporarily down shows a blurred placeholder. Try again later; the puzzle itself is saved.

### A picture is wrong, offensive or should not be there

Send us the pack and puzzle name. We check every report and remove pictures that do not meet the licence or content bar.

### How do I delete my account and data?

In the app: Profile > Account > Delete account (guests too). It deletes the account, profile, progress, purchase records, friends and uploaded photos straight away, on every device, and the game starts over as a new guest. If you signed in with Apple you confirm with Apple once more. A VIP subscription is not cancelled by this: cancel it in your App Store subscriptions first. You can also write to us from the email on your account (or with your friend code if you play as a guest) and we delete everything within 30 days. See the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).

### The widget shows an old picture

Widgets refresh on Apple's schedule, usually within an hour of midnight. Opening the app refreshes it immediately.

## Reporting a bug

Include the app version (Profile > Settings, bottom of the screen), your iPhone model and iOS version, and what you did just before the problem. Screenshots help.
