Version 1.7.0

Card Jigsaw World is now Carden, your calm picture puzzle, with a new icon: a lotus in coloured glass. Your progress, coins and packs stay as they are.

New ways to play
- Painting: cards start in black and white and get their colour back as they lock. Pays 25% more.
- Giant boards of 100 to 300 cards, with pinch to zoom, an overview map that shows where a tapped card belongs, Gather (which shuffles the loose cards when they are all in view), and a board that opens where you left it. Magic puts the selected card in its place.
- Peek costs 10 coins: every card flies to its place for a moment, then back.
- Five Giant packs of detailed pictures made for the biggest boards: Colourful Houses, Painted Paradises, Market Stalls, Curiosity Shops and Treasure Trove.
- Shapes and options open as you level up: Squares, Triangles, Hexagons, Painting, then giant boards, each with a short intro.

Quests and rewards
- Quests that do not overlap: besides Solve 3 puzzles, each one asks for something different, like a shape you rarely play, a bigger board, Painting, a board at par, a time to beat or cards locked in one move.
- Claim all on the Quests tab, a dot on the tab when a reward is ready, and the completion screen tells you when a quest is done.
- Lucky Wheel: some coin prizes are now Peeks and Magic, kept for your next games and used before coins. See odds lists every prize and its chance.
- VIP members get their 500 bonus coins automatically once a week, and VIP can be shared with your family through Family Sharing.

Home and Library
- Quick play opens the mode and board picker with your last board selected, and Another picture draws a new one.
- Once today's daily puzzle is solved, Home shows tomorrow's as a blurred preview.
- Library search finds pictures by title, new packs carry a New badge, and there are Finished, New and Hide finished filters.
- Home shows your packs in progress, packs missing stars and finished packs.

Reminders and account
- Daily reminders: turn them on in Settings and choose the time. None on days you have already claimed your daily gift.
- New events: a reminder the evening before a world-day event opens, if you have not played for a while.
- Delete your account in the app: Profile > Account > Delete account.
- Report or block a player from their page; blocked players leave your leaderboard.
- Settings: Rate Carden, the real app version, and Redeem a pack code for special packs. Dark and tinted Home Screen icons on iOS 18.
- Links to packs, pictures, player profiles, the store and pack codes open the matching screen, in the app and on the web site.

Pictures and sync
- New Illustration packs: Big Buck Bunny, Fantasy Worlds, Pantry Shelves, Tinker's Workshop, Garden Plots and House and Home.
- Every picture is served from our own servers, and the catalogue is rebuilt: no people, no repeats, portrait or square only, at least 20 pictures per pack, new AI and Abstract categories.
- One save on every device: progress from the iPhone and the web site adds up, play made offline is kept, and records only improve.
- The Social tab shows one leaderboard, ranked by total XP; it and My Puzzles load more as you scroll.
- My Puzzles photos are private to your account. Free players keep up to 5 photos; VIP has no limit.
- Finishing a puzzle pays fewer coins. Six starter packs, one per category, are free; the other library packs cost coins, and any pack you have already played stays open.
