Version 1.7.0

- Every picture is now served from our own servers, so tiles and covers load reliably.
- The picture catalogue is rebuilt: no people, no repeats, portrait or square only, at least 20 pictures per pack, and new AI and Abstract categories.
- Finishing a puzzle pays fewer coins; packs stay at their prices.
- Dragged pieces show every detail: square corners and no frame.
- Offers in an ad break open their destination instead of the next puzzle.
- Cleaner board preview corners on the difficulty screen.
- VIP members get their 500 bonus coins automatically once a week.
- Daily gift: today's day is outlined instead of filled, so the coin, ticket and hint icons stay easy to read.
- Every pack in the catalogue now shows in the Library, including the AI and Abstract categories.
- Upcoming content stays hidden until it is close: daily months appear on their first day, events three days before they open.
- My Puzzles photos are private to your account. Free players keep up to 5 photos; VIP has no limit.
- The league and My Puzzles load more as you scroll; players with equal XP share a place.
- Quick play on Home starts a random picture you have not played yet.
- Home shows your packs in progress, packs missing stars and finished packs.
- Library: a Finished filter, a Hide finished switch and search.
- Profiles show Total Completion and quests completed.
- Tickets on blue buttons are now white, so prices are easy to read.
