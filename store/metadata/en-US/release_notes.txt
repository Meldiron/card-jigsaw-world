Version 1.7.0

- New ticket icon and cobalt accent.
- Puzzles never start with a piece already in place; loose pieces are slightly dimmed and light up as they lock.
- Joined pieces fuse into one shape with a blue outline, in every mode.
- "View picture" on the finish screen shows your completed photo in full.
- Signing in to an existing account now merges your progress; when both saves matter you choose which coins and level to keep.
- Fully three-starred packs get a star badge next to the check.
- Break offers are rarer and close faster.
- Also playable on the web at card-jigsaw-world.appwrite.network (purchases stay on iPhone).
