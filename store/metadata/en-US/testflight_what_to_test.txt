Thanks for testing Card Jigsaw World 1.7.0. Please try:

1. Play today's daily puzzle from Home and finish it.
2. Open Library, pick a pack and play one board in each mode: Cards, Squares, Triangles, Hexagons.
3. Drag a card next to its neighbour so they snap into a group, then move the group.
4. Open the Store (tap the coin balance on Home) and start the VIP free trial with your Sandbox Apple ID. The VIP badge should appear on Home within a few seconds.
5. Profile > Settings > Account: Sign in with Apple. Your coins and progress should stay.
6. Add the Card Jigsaw World widget to your Home Screen and tap it; it should open today's puzzle.
7. Long-press the app icon and tap "One last game?".

Anything that crashes, freezes, shows a wrong balance or a picture that does not load: use the TestFlight feedback button with a screenshot.
