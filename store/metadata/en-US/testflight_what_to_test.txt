Thanks for testing Carden 2.0. Please try:

1. Play today's daily puzzle from Home and finish it.
2. Open the Library, pick a pack and play a board in Squares or with Painting. Then open another picture: it should start on the same choice.
3. Drag a card next to its neighbour so they join into a group, then move the group.
4. Profile > Settings > Accessibility: try a larger text size, Reduce motion, Increase contrast and Differentiate without colour on a few screens.
5. Open the Store (tap the coin balance on Home) and start the VIP free trial with your Sandbox Apple ID. The VIP badge should appear within a few seconds.
6. Profile > Settings > Sign in or create account: Sign in with Apple. Your coins and progress should stay.
7. Add the Carden widget to your Home Screen and tap it; it should open today's puzzle.
8. Tap a play.carden.page link in Notes or Messages: Carden should open on that screen.

Anything that crashes, freezes, shows a wrong balance or a picture that does not load: use the TestFlight feedback button with a screenshot.
