What is new since the last build:
- A new board opens on its whole picture for a moment, then the cards deal out (tap to skip). A tap during Peek ends it at once.
- Quests: swipe a quest left to swap it for 1 ticket; every quest you have not finished can be swapped, and timed quests ask for a time you can beat.
- Home: the Today card says when the next picture comes (the Tomorrow row is gone). The Inbox has a calmer message sheet, Shuffle has room for its name, and the pause menu says "Discard and pick a board".
- Cards that look the same now also count on dark pictures (night skies, black backgrounds). Friend gifts: up to 20 a day, with a notification only between friends who added each other.

Please try: open a few new boards and watch the picture deal out, swipe a quest left, and play a night picture at 7x7.
