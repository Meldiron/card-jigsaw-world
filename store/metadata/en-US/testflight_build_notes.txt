What is new since the last build:
- Play together: whoever grabs a card first keeps it, the other player's card slides back with a short note, and a drag never gets stuck. Invite links now show the real picture and board in the message preview and open the app directly.
- New board sizes: Triangles 3x3 and Hexagons 4x4 and 6x6. A few edge pieces of a unique shape start in place, so the picture stays a full rectangle.
- Night photos: dark sky cards that differ only by a star or two now count as the same card.

Please try: send a Play together invite in Messages and check the preview, then both grab the same card at once.
