New in this build:
- New app icon: the Carden lotus in coloured glass, with matching dark and tinted versions for the Home Screen. The web site and its tab icon use it too.
- A board you finished on another device stays finished: if an older copy is still open somewhere (a browser tab, a second phone), it closes with "Solved on another device" instead of coming back as Continue.
- Sync notices and the update prompt no longer appear in the middle of a game.

Please try: the new icon on your Home Screen in light, dark and tinted mode, and finishing a board on one device while it is still open on another.
