What is new since the last build:
- Play together is live: when a friend picks up a card you see it at once in their colour, and when they drag it the card glides across your board as they move it.
- Opening an invite link in Safari: Open in Carden now switches to the app.
- Quests count each board as it was finished, so replaying a picture no longer changes them. The web version loads faster on later visits.

Please try: start Play together, have a friend join, and both drag cards around the same board.
