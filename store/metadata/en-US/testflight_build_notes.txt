New in this build:
- Daily reminders work: turn them on in Settings > Daily reminders and choose the time (19:00 by default). One a day with the daily puzzle, none on days you have already claimed your daily gift.
- Quick play opens the mode and board picker instead of starting a game; your last board is selected.
- The Quests tab shows a dot while a quest or event reward is waiting to be claimed.
- Social shows a plain leaderboard ranked by total XP (no league or season).

Please try: turn on Daily reminders, set the time a few minutes ahead, leave the game, and tap the reminder when it arrives.
