What is new since the last build:
- Play together: in a board's pause menu, Play together shares a link. Up to five people play the same board live, in the app or in a browser, each in their own colour. The players button at the top lists everyone; the host can remove a player or end the game.
- Friend gifts: once a day, send a friend 1 ticket or 150 coins from their page. It arrives in their Inbox with a notification on iPhone and waits 24 hours. Settings > Gifts from friends turns the notifications off.
- Boards are square now, 3x3 to 7x7 (Expand plays the whole picture in its own shape). Shuffle replaces Undo, Magic places a card and its neighbours, a group you push moves aside whole, and cards that look the same count as placed.
- Lucky Wheel ×5 spins, booster bundles (Peeks, Hints and Magic), piggy bank upgrades up to 1,000 coins, and Swap on a quest for 1 ticket. Level-ups pay up to 300 coins. Settings > Privacy > Share usage data.

Please try: start Play together on a board and open the link on a second device or in Safari, then move cards on both. Add a friend by code and send them a gift.
