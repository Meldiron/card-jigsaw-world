New in this build:
- One save on every device: coins, unlocks and solved pictures from the iPhone and the web site now add up instead of overwriting each other. Play made offline is kept and sent when the connection returns.
- The save reloads when you open the app or return to it, and a short note lists what arrived from another device. If the same picture has two different boards in progress, you choose which one to keep.
- Records only improve, and replaying a solved picture no longer pays the first-solve reward again.
- The picture catalogue loads faster: after the first launch only new or changed packs are downloaded.

Please try: sign in with the same account on the iPhone and on card-jigsaw-world.appwrite.network, solve a picture on one, then open the other.
