New in this build (giant boards):
- Locate is always on: tap a card and the overview map shows where it belongs (when the map is on). The Locate switch and the zoom hint are gone.
- Gather is in the bar at every zoom. When every card for the view is already here or nearby, Gather shuffles the loose cards instead; cards in place and joined groups stay where they are.
- No more double tap to zoom: use pinch or the zoom buttons. Magic now puts the selected card in its place.

Please try: a Colossal board, Gather on the whole board, and Magic with a card selected.
