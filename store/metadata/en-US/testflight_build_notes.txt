New in this build:
- Report and block: a player's page has Report (offensive name, cheating, something else) and Block. Blocked players leave your leaderboard; unblock them in Settings > Blocked players.
- Links: cardjigsaw:// links open the matching screen (a pack, a picture, a profile, the store, a pack code filled in), and the same paths work on the web site. Redeem a pack code is no longer in Settings on iPhone; codes open through their link.
- Store: when the App Store had no products at launch, opening the store or tapping a bundle asks again. Terms of use and Privacy policy are linked under VIP and in Settings.
- Notes before new events are now off until you turn them on in Settings.

Please try: report and block a player from the leaderboard, then unblock them in Settings; and cardjigsaw://daily typed into Safari's address bar.
