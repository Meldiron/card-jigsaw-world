Carden is your calm picture puzzle.

Every photo is cut into cards and shuffled across the board. Tap a card, then the place it belongs, or drag it there. Cards that belong together snap into groups and move as one. A card in its true place locks into the picture, until the whole photo is back. Fewer moves earn more stars.

WAYS TO PLAY
- Cards, squares, triangles and hexagons: four ways to cut every picture, each with its own ladder from a quick 3x3 to a board that fills an evening.
- Painting: cards start in black and white and take their colour back as they lock.
- Giant boards of 100 to 300 cards, with pinch to zoom and a map of the whole picture.
- A daily puzzle every day, with monthly packs back to January 2023. The current month is free.
- Packs of museum paintings, wildlife, deep space, cities, seasons and more. Every picture names its author and licence.
- Live events for world days, each with its own pack for a few days around the date.
- Your own photos, turned into puzzles.

HELP WHEN YOU WANT IT
Peek shows where every card belongs for a moment. Hint and Magic help with a stubborn card. Undo is always free, and every board waits for you to come back.

PROGRESS
- Earn coins, tickets and hints as you play: levels, daily and weekly quests, a daily gift and a piggy bank that fills with every puzzle.
- Spin the Lucky Wheel, collect avatars and achievements, and compare progress with friends on the leaderboard.

ON IPHONE
- Home Screen widgets with today's picture.
- "One last game?" on the app icon opens today's puzzle.
- Light and dark mode. Music, sounds and haptics can each be turned off.

NO ACCOUNT NEEDED
Start as a guest in one tap. Sign in with Apple or with email when you want your progress on more than one device.

VIP
VIP opens every pack, including past months, adds a daily and a weekly quest, pays 500 bonus coins every week, allows unlimited My Puzzles photos and turns off the game's own promotions. VIP can be shared with your family through Family Sharing. Monthly and yearly plans both begin with a 7-day free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple ID account when the trial ends or at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings.

No third-party ads, no tracking, no analytics SDKs.
