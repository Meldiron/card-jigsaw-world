Thank you for reviewing Carden.

Carden was called Card Jigsaw World in earlier TestFlight builds. Only the name changed; the bundle ID and every feature are the same.

ACCOUNTS
No account is required. The first launch creates an anonymous guest session on our backend (Appwrite, EU region) and the whole game is playable immediately. No demo credentials are needed. Sign in with Apple and email sign-up are optional and only move the same guest progress onto a permanent account (Profile > Settings > Account).

PURCHASES
In-app purchases are StoreKit 2 consumables (coins, tickets, hints) and two auto-renewable subscriptions in the "VIP" group (vip_monthly, vip_yearly), both with a 7-day free introductory offer. Nothing is granted on the device: each finished transaction is sent to our Appwrite Function "verify-purchase", which verifies Apple's signed transaction (JWS) against Apple Root CA G3 and applies the grant once. During review the function runs in the Sandbox environment, so sandbox purchases made with a Sandbox Apple ID verify end to end and credit the account within a few seconds.

How to test a purchase: on Home tap the coin balance (or any "Get coins" / "Go VIP" card) to open the Store, choose "2,500 coins" or "Go VIP", and confirm with a Sandbox Apple ID. The balance at the top of the screen updates after verification. "Restore purchases" is in Profile > Settings > Account.

ADS
There is no ad network and no third-party SDK for ads or analytics. The "rewarded video" and interstitial cards are house ads: short, closable promotions of the game's own in-app store. They never appear for VIP players.

CONTENT
Catalog photos come from Openverse (CC0, public domain, CC BY, CC BY-SA), the Cleveland Museum of Art Open Access API (CC0), the NASA Image and Video Library and Lorem Picsum (Unsplash License). Author and licence are shown on every finished puzzle. Players can also upload their own photos (My Puzzles); these are private to the uploader and not shared with other players.

SOCIAL
The leaderboard shows display names and avatars only. Friends are added by friend code. There is no chat, no messaging and no user-generated text beyond the display name.
